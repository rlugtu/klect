## Description (4000 max)

Klect is where your bookmarks finally make sense. Save a link and Klect fills in the name, description and photos from the page, so one paste becomes a rich card you can browse, tag, rate and add notes to.

Keep your saves in lists and share them with friends. Invite people as viewers who can browse and comment, or as collaborators who can add and edit alongside you. Plan a trip, rank the best tacos, or collect research together, with comments on every bookmark, a group chat for each list, and polls to settle the vote.

What you get:
- Paste to save: Klect pulls the title, description and images from any link.
- Shared lists: invite friends as viewers or collaborators.
- Comments, group chat and polls on your lists.
- Tags with their own colors, so lists are easy to scan and filter.
- Near me: find the places you saved close to where you are right now.
- Direct messages with friends, and bookmarks you can send in a chat.
- Public lists and profiles you can share with a link.
- Your style: modern, journal and pixel themes, each in light and dark.

Klect is free to start. The free plan includes up to 3 lists of your own and 20 bookmarks. Klect Pro removes those limits. Joining and collaborating on other people's lists is always free.

Klect Pro subscription
- Klect Pro Monthly: $1.99 per month
- Klect Pro Annual: $19.99 per year
Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription in your Apple Account settings.
